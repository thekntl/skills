# KNTL Legal

The last face before production. Four documents, terms of use, subscription, support, and privacy/KVKK, each published as a public **base** text the product adopts unchanged plus an **addendum** listing the clauses this product changes, adds, or removes. Owner voice, ticket and PR bodies, Hikaye, ledger citations, and owner-only steps follow `docs/agents/kntl-conventions.md`. Everything lands in `docs/legal/` of the product repo: `data-provider-inventory.md`, `base-<document>.md` (research findings and the Base line, one per distinct base), `<document>.base.<lang>.md` (the base copied verbatim, only when it cannot be linked), `<document>.addendum.<lang>.md`, `to-questionnaire-<trigger>.md`, with `<document>` one of `terms`, `subscription`, `support`, `privacy`.

## Timing

The gate belongs to `/kntl-next`: start once it has reported "Production'a hazırız" and the owner said yes; lacking that, reply "Hukuk production'dan hemen önce gelir; önce `/kntl-next`." and stop. Work inside one ticket titled "Legal texts", labelled `kntl:legal`, `flow:<scenario-slug>` of the scenario whose screen first shows the texts (the purchase scenario when the product sells, otherwise the one that links them), `ready-for-agent` while drafting and `ready-for-human` from the publication checklist on: open it when absent, claim it, and record each document the product has no use for (a free product needs no subscription text) under Out of scope on the map.

## Steps

1. **Inventory.** Copy `assets/data-provider-inventory.template.md` to `docs/legal/data-provider-inventory.md`. One row per provider in `docs/agents/kntl-stack.md` and per adapter, SDK, OS permission string, and outbound call in the code. A product fact the repo cannot answer goes through the Skill tool with `kntl-grilling`; the answer lands in the ledger with `"face":"legal"`. Done when every provider and adapter has a row with every column filled or `none`, and every product-fact line cites a file path or a `D-` id.
2. **Triggers.** Fill the inventory's trigger table. Done when each of the four triggers reads yes or no with its evidence, and each yes has a lawyer packet: call the Skill tool with `to-questionnaire` (recipient: the lawyer; needed back: one answer per yes row), saving to `docs/legal/to-questionnaire-<trigger>.md`, its path in the row's Lawyer answer column until the answer replaces it. Drafting continues; publication waits for the answers.
3. **Base texts.** Read `references/base-texts.md` before choosing or citing any base. Once per distinct base (`subscription` and `support` take the `terms` base and cite `docs/legal/base-terms.md` and its Base line), call the Skill tool with `research` to verify the candidate against its primary source and the constraints the reference lists, findings to `docs/legal/base-<document>.md`. Done when each applicable document has a Base line whose name, publisher, version, date, retrieval date, and URL were copied from the page the findings cite or from a page opened in this session, and every clause of the base resolves to one number, the numbering recorded in the findings file when the base prints none.
4. **Addenda.** Copy `assets/addendum.template.md` to `docs/legal/<document>.addendum.<lang>.md` per applicable document and per language the inventory lists, keeping the template's fixed sentences in that language. Walk the base clause by clause. Done when every clause that misdescribes the product has an entry (Changed, Added, or Removed) whose comment cites the inventory row or `D-` id behind it, and every inventory fact the base leaves uncovered (provider, region, retention, user right, price, renewal, restore, support channel, age gate) appears in exactly one entry.
5. **Consistency.** Compare the addenda with every surface that states a legal fact: paywall copy, store listing and App Privacy answers, web checkout, settings and about links, landing footer, support entry. Done when each surface links to its text and states the same facts; each mismatch is a ticket on the face that owns the surface.
6. **Publication checklist.** Commit `docs/legal/*` on `main` as `docs: legal texts` and push, directly, the way `/kntl-handoff` commits; repeat once the Approval block is filled. Comment on the ticket, per document: Base line, addendum path, language, effective date, destination (page URL, store field, in-app screen), lawyer status. Open one wiring ticket per destination that has to carry a text (web page, store field, in-app link), labelled with the surface owner's face, its scenario's flow, and triage per the conventions file. Ask the owner for "yayın onayı" as a one-question round by calling the Skill tool with `kntl-grilling` on the Legal texts ticket; its `D-` id goes into the inventory's Approval block and is what `/kntl-release` reads. Close the Legal texts ticket with its Hikaye per the conventions file; the wiring tickets stay open on their own faces. Done when the approval's `D-` id reads `confirmed` in the ledger and sits in the Approval block, every yes trigger carries the lawyer's answer, every destination on the checklist has its wiring ticket, `docs/legal/*` is on `origin/main`, and the ticket is closed with its Hikaye. Last line "Sıradaki için `/kntl-next`."
