# KNTL Marketing

Runs the marketing face (`kntl:marketing`) and writes the briefs the landing face (`kntl:landing`) is built from: niche → territory → destination → measurement → creative → routine, so the first campaign is ready before production and goes live the day the product is public. The golden rule for every word and frame: sell the user's truthful outcome; features are proof. Owner-facing formats, tool access and owner-only limits come from `docs/agents/kntl-conventions.md`, read before writing anything; providers with their resolved access paths, the ad-account topology and the paid learning channel come from `docs/agents/kntl-stack.md`. Direction choices are made by calling the Skill tool with `kntl-grilling`.

Bare, a run charts the face: steps 1–5, starting at the first whose Done line fails, then Hand back. With `#N`, a run does the step that ticket's `## What to build` names, within the ticket's acceptance criteria, resolves the ticket as `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` says, then Hand back. Artefacts live under `docs/marketing/` and are linked from their tickets.

## Preflight

1. The POC ticket is closed on the map and `docs/design/SCENARIOS.md` exists, so creatives can be shot from the real UI; otherwise "Önce App Shell ve POC bitmeli." and stop.
2. `docs/agents/kntl-stack.md` has rows for analytics, payment, ASO, SEO, the marketing site and ad accounts, plus attribution for a native app; a missing row → "Önce `/kntl-setup`." and stop.

## Steps

1. **Niches.** From the map, the ledger and the scenario catalogue, call the Skill tool with `kntl-grilling` on who has the problem the promise solves; several niches may survive. Done when each surviving niche is a ledger entry (`face: "marketing"`, tags `["niche", …]`) naming persona, before state, after state and reachable channels, its rank in the entry's `scope` (`niş 1/3, ilk lansman`), and the ranked list sits under the map's Decisions so far.
2. **Competitor evidence.** Read `references/competitor-evidence.md` before proposing the shortlist; it holds the classification, the shortlist flow, the evidence row and the cluster file. Done when every owner-confirmed competitor has a research file with sourced rows, `docs/marketing/competitor-evidence.md` names an opportunity or "none" per cluster and lists coverage gaps; a market-creating product records that classification on the map instead.
3. **Territories.** Per ranked niche, write three materially different value-led territories and grill them. Done when each niche has one winning territory with its ledger id, and a brief copied from `assets/campaign-brief.template.md` to `docs/marketing/campaigns/<slug>.md` with Business frame, Persona and change, and Territories filled.
4. **Destinations.** Name each brief's destination: store page, existing URL, or a landing page. A landing page gets the brief's Landing section filled; `/kntl-design` (web) designs it from that section and `docs/design/DESIGN.md`; `/kntl-implement` builds it from the `kntl:landing` ticket filed in step 5. Done when every brief's Destination resolves to one of the three, every landing page has a filled Landing section, and the map records the landing face's size: the pages step 5 will file, or the landing face shrunk to the store page: one `kntl:landing` ticket for the store page copy and its conversion event, and the map's Notes line for the face reads `küçültüldü: store page only` with the reason.
5. **Ticket the face.** Call the Skill tool with `to-tickets` on the briefs: one ticket per Landing, Measurement and Production item, one per brief to read its Results row after the learning window, one for the routine, one per account, identity, budget and activation the owner holds; the issue body and triage labels come from the conventions file and override its template. Every ticket carries `kntl:marketing` (landing pages `kntl:landing`; wiring inside product code `kntl:platform`) and the brief's `flow:` slug; face-wide tickets (routine, accounts, identities, budgets) take the first-ranked niche's brief's slug. Tickets this skill performs (steps 6–8; Results belongs to step 7) open `## What to build` with "Step <n> · `/kntl-marketing #N`"; `kntl:landing` and `kntl:platform` tickets open with `/kntl-implement #N` and cite the brief path and section (`docs/marketing/campaigns/<slug>.md` › Landing, or › Business frame › Conversion). Owner-held items are `ready-for-human`, filed first as one batch; the rest `ready-for-agent`, blocked natively on the human tickets they wait for. Done when every brief's Landing, Measurement, Production and Results items and the routine map to a ticket.
6. **Measurement.** Wire each brief's Conversion to its named event in the analytics tool, the attribution link in the MMP for a native app, and the purchase event from the payment tool, as the stack file describes; dashboards, links and campaign objects are made here. Done when one test session on `staging` shows the conversion in every tool with the campaign's tracking parameters attached.
7. **Creatives.** Read `references/creative-loop.md` before the first frame; it holds the five answers, the campaign chain, the footage rule, the production order, approvals and the paid-learning gate. Done when the brief's Production table lists every asset by path with its footage scenarios, and the owner approved each representative asset.
8. **Routine and publishing.** Copy `assets/content-routine.template.md` to `docs/marketing/content-routine.md` and fill the boundaries, then act as creative-loop.md › Approval and › Paid learning gate say. Done when the boundaries carry the owner's yes, the first posts are approved, and every paid campaign is in the state creative-loop.md's Approval allows.
9. **Hand back.** Answer in Turkish: what now exists, what the owner does next (each human ticket with its exact step), then `Ticket sözlüğü` and "Sıradaki için `/kntl-next`."; a `#N` run ends with the conventions file's Hikaye. Done when the message names every human ticket.

## Face done

Marketing: the first campaign's creatives are approved, its conversion fires end to end on `staging`, the routine carries the owner's yes, and launch day needs only the owner's activation. Landing: every campaign destination that is a page is published with its conversion event firing, or, when the face was shrunk, the store page carries the campaign's conversion event.
