# Competitor evidence

Read before proposing the shortlist. Purpose: what users of the category repeatedly struggle with, so territories and product opportunities rest on evidence.

## Classify first

A market-creating or reframing product records that classification under the map's Notes and skips the shortlist; its territories come from the niche interviews alone. A product in an existing category gets a shortlist.

## Shortlist

1. The owner defines the category, the direction, seed competitors and the leaders.
2. Extend with missed competitors from the ASO tools `docs/agents/kntl-stack.md` names (same category, adjacent, keyword neighbours), each marked provisional.
3. The owner confirms the shortlist; only confirmed rows are researched.

## Research each confirmed competitor

Call the Skill tool with `research` once per competitor, leaders first, each call writing `docs/marketing/competitors/<slug>.md`. Sources: store reviews, social discussions, forums, communities and review sites the agent can reach. Stop expanding a competitor's sources when a new source adds no new cluster.

Evidence row: competitor · source · URL · date · locale · user context · evidence (quote or paraphrase) · direct or inferred · type (product gap, usability, reliability, price, trust, support, unmet outcome) · severity signal · workaround · confidence. Praise and table stakes get their own rows: what users would miss.

## Cluster into `docs/marketing/competitor-evidence.md`

```markdown
# Competitor evidence — <codename>

## Confirmed shortlist
| Competitor | Role (leader / direct / adjacent) | Markets and locales | Priority |

## Pain-point clusters
| Cluster | Personas | Frequency | Severity | Recency | Confidence | Product opportunity or none | Ticket or ledger id |

## Praise and table stakes
| Pattern | Evidence | Keep or learn |

## Coverage gaps
- <source that could not be reached, market without evidence>
```

A cluster's opportunity links to a ticket on the map or a ledger entry, or says "none"; a gap stays listed until a later run closes it.
